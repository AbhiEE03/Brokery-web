# Benchmark 2026-09-29

Same dataset and requests measured twice: with only `_id` indexes, then with the schema indexes.
Latencies in milliseconds (autocannon, 10 connections, 6s per scenario, single run).

**Dataset:** 21 users, 100,000 clients, 30,000 properties, 278,162 stageTransitions, 50,000 matches, 5,000 changeRequests, 100,000 activityLogs.
Broker scenarios use one of 20 brokers (~5,000 clients each).

**Machine:** AMD Ryzen 5 5600H with Radeon Graphics (12 threads), 15 GB RAM, Windows_NT 10.0.26200, Node v23.11.0, MongoDB 8.2.6 (in-memory replica set on the same machine).

| Clients by stage (admin) | 237 | 363 | 44 | 60 | 5.4× | 40 → 224 |
